## BOOK-02e · 2026-11-08 · registry — a party is entered only where it is known (#441, contract #449, claim #450)

**Did** — A party is registered in an entrance, and only that entrance's book may name it. `registry.party` gains `entrance_id` (migration `V202611081620`). Existing parties are tied to the one entrance whose book names them. A party named in two entrances' books makes the migration fail, naming the party. A party no book names keeps NULL, and no book can name it. A CHECK `NOT VALID` requires the entrance on every new row, and composite FKs `(party_id, entrance_id) → party(id, entrance_id)` back the service checks on `title` and `household_member`. `POST /api/registry/parties` takes a required `entranceId`. It is decided for that entrance (`decideInAny` over that one entrance, no matrix change), and an unknown entrance is a 404 after the policy. `assignTitle` and `registerHousehold` answer a party of another entrance exactly as an unknown one. Both answer the 400 `PM-BOOK-011: no party <id>`: for a title an unknown party was a 404, for a member it was `PM-BOOK-002: no party <id>`. The import's owner parties are created in their entrance. The web party form asks for the entrance's number. `seed_demo.py` and `check_e2e.py` pass it. The catalogue lines cite PM-BOOK-011; `openapi.json` and `schema.d.ts` are regenerated.

**Rules covered** — PM-BOOK-011 (an id from elsewhere names nobody in this book and tells nothing), PM-BOOK-002 (the book's members and holders are its own entrance's parties).
