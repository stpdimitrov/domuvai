**Did** — `POST …/assemblies/{a}/agenda/{item}/votes` (`VoteService`, `VoteActs`, `VoteController`). At the sitting that opened, on its day, whoever runs the assembly records how a present owner voted on an item of this agenda. Present means on the register in person, by video once identified, or brought by a counted proxy's representative; the channel says which. There is one row per unit they own that day, weighed by their title share of its ideal parts, exactly. A second vote is a correction: a new row per unit reversing the one in effect, saying why. A new action, `RECORD_VOTE` (matrix v19), goes to the manager, the board, the controller and an owner, and it must also be theirs to run, as with the register. The migration `V202610240810__vote_recorded.sql` makes the weight exact numeric, adds the assembly, the sitting, who recorded the vote and their sign-in, and the correction, and replaces the unique constraint with `vote_first_once` and `vote_reversed_once`.
