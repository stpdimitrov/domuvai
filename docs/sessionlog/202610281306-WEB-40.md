## WEB-40 · 2026-10-28 · web — a unit's statement and arrears

**Did** — mode BOTH. A new screen, `/entrance/statement` ("Сметка на обект" in the sidebar). A GET form picks a unit from the registry's units list and a date (`?asOf=`, today in Sofia unless given). The screen then reads `GET /api/money/units/{unitId}/arrears` and shows the total, advance and net, the ageing bands in the API's order, and the oldest open debt's due date. It also reads `GET /api/money/units/{unitId}/statement` and shows every line by period with its key, count and derivation, consumption lines apart, and the balance. Every figure, band and due date comes from the API; the web computes none. A unit that is not in the entrance's list is not shown under it. A refusal is shown in the API's words. Each debtor row on `/debts` links to that unit's statement as of the date `/debts` reads.
