## AUD-02 · 2026-10-11 · identity-org — one reading of "successor": the recording side and the reading side ask the same function (audit finding 5)

**Did** — the audit found two readings of PM-GOV-004's "until a successor is elected" in one module: recording a mandate never ended a board member still in term, while reading who holds an office ended an expired member on *any* later executive mandate — so member A (2024-01-01 → 2026-01-01) lost the office on 2026-01-01 because B had been by-elected in March 2025, though nobody was elected to succeed A. The owner decided the reading ("QA 1"), and it is now said once, in `Mandate.succeeds`: a later manager succeeds every earlier executive mandate of the entrance; a later board member succeeds an earlier manager, and an earlier member only when it begins on or after the day that member's term ends. Recording a mandate asks it to find what the new one ends; `MandateRoles` asks it to find whether somebody took an expired mandate's place. The SQL that held the recording side's copy is gone — `MandateStore.ofEntrance` reads the entrance's mandates for both. A is a board member on 2026-01-01 and after, until a successor or an end is recorded. No migration, no endpoint.

**Rules covered** — PM-GOV-004 (the continuation; its acceptance — `EXPIRED_ACTING`, the election task — is still not built).

Eight mutants killed: the old reading (any later executive mandate) · the boundary day · a manager not succeeding a member in term · a member not succeeding a manager · the reader ignoring an unrecorded successor · a successor not yet started · another entrance's · the recorder ending a firm's mandate.

**Review** — fresh context, seven points; one reading remains, with no copy in SQL or Kotlin. **Decisions** — the owner's, through the coordinator, 2026-10-11 ("QA 1"): a board member past term continues until a recorded end, a later sole manager, or a member whose mandate begins on or after that member's own last day; a by-election during the term succeeds nobody.
