## GOV-01 · 2026-10-12 · identity-org + law — a mandate's standing on a date: in term, an election due, acting past term (PM-GOV-004's acceptance — the status only)

**Did** — the owner accepted that an expired board member keeps the office until a successor or an end is recorded (AUD-02); nothing could say that such a member exists. Now something can. `Mandate.standingOn(date, …)` derives, for a mandate in force on a date: **IN_TERM**; **ELECTION_DUE**, once the date is within the reminder period of the day the office is left and nobody is on record to follow, with the day to elect by; **EXPIRED_ACTING**, from the day the term ends for as long as nobody has taken the place — each with `until`, the first day the mandate is no longer in force where the record says. It rests on the mandate's own dates and the one reading of "successor" (`Mandate.succeeds`); nothing is stored, so nothing can disagree with the mandate. The reminder period is a dated constant in `law`, `MANDATE_ELECTION_REMINDER_DAYS`, marked unconfirmed — it is the catalogue's reminder, not the statute's — and the value in force on the day the office is left is the one applied. `MandateStanding.of(entrance, date)` lists every mandate in force with its standing. **In-process only: no endpoint serves it, no screen shows it, and no task is raised from it.**

**Rules covered** — PM-GOV-004: the `EXPIRED_ACTING` status of its acceptance. **Not** "an election task is raised", and **not** PM-GOV-005 — nothing is scheduled and no test carries that ID.

**Tests added** — `MandateStandingTest` (no Spring, no database): each boundary day for the manager and the board, with one date pinned by hand (a term ending 2026-03-01 is due from 2025-12-31) · a successor on record, in time and late · a resignation with nobody elected · the audit's by-election case · the controller and the cashier · a mandate shorter than the period · over every office, with a successor, an end, both or neither, on every day of four years: a standing exactly when in force, and `until` the first day it is not · the read · `PM-SYS-002 the reminder period is the one in force on the day the office is left…`. `LawTest`: the figure pinned, unconfirmed. `MandateRolesIT` (from a scratch copy on this machine's Postgres 16, 3 of 3): the read and the roles agree on who is in force on seven dates. Ten mutants killed, among them never acting · the period ignored · the period read as at the day asked · a recorded end with nobody elected silencing the election · an election due though a successor is on record · a reminder invented where the law holds none.

**Review** — fresh context, seven points. Taken: a bare recorded end counted as "the election has been held", so a resignation with nobody elected was never reported — only a mandate that succeeds this one silences now; a seat that names nobody was dropped from the read — it is listed; `until` was a day in the past where a manager was recorded mid-term — it is the day the mandate really stops being in force, and a test holds it to that on every day; a period the law does not hold was replaced by a zero written here — now none is given, and an unknown constant stops the application at start; the test and the comments claimed PM-GOV-005 and named callers that do not exist — they say what is not built; one date is pinned by hand, so the test no longer only repeats the implementation's arithmetic.

**Decisions** — mine, stated on #202: the reminder period is not a statutory figure — unconfirmed, in `law` · it is read as at the day the office is left, and counts back from the day the term ends, the first day outside it · a recorded end with nobody to follow does not silence the election · a mandate naming no party is listed. **Asked of the owner through the coordinator, not answered yet:** where the election task lives — a derived read with an endpoint (which needs a line in the policy matrix), the `compliance` module now, or neither.

**Open** — **the acceptance's "an election task is raised" is not met**, and nobody is told anything until an endpoint or a task exists: that waits on the owner's answer and, for an endpoint, on the `policy` lane · PM-GOV-005 (scheduling the election, the countdown) is not built · a vacant office — after a resignation, or a seat nobody was elected to — has no mandate and so no standing: this read cannot show it · one new board member beginning on the day several members' terms end is on record as following all of them, and silences the election for every such seat — the table has no seats (`docs/RULES.md` §7, question 11) · the reminder period is unconfirmed (`TODO(legal)`) · the control board's continuation is question 12.
