## WEB-39 · 2026-10-28 · web — a payment recorded and its allocation shown

**Did** — mode BOTH. A new screen, `/entrance/payments` ("Плащания" in the sidebar). Its form records a payment already received: the unit (from the registry's units list), the amount in euros, the value date (defaulting to today in Sofia), the account it went into (OPERATING · REPAIR_RENEWAL · CASH), and optionally the debt date the payer designated. It posts `POST /api/money/entrances/{entranceId}/payments`. On success it redirects to `?payment=<id>`, where `GET …/payments/{paymentId}` is read and shown: the rule applied (OLDEST_FIRST | DESIGNATED, in words and as the code), what each debt received, and what was left unallocated. A refusal is shown in the API's words, with the form kept.

The Idempotency-Key is minted once per form render. A refusal recorded nothing, so the form comes back under a new key. A lost answer keeps the key, and the person is told to send the form again unchanged. The screen says it records money received and moves none (ADR-007 stays unbuilt). Every figure comes from the API, shown with `eur()`; euros typed become minor units through `minorOf`.
