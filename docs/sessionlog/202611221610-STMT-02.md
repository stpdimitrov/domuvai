## STMT-02 · 2026-11-22 · a statement line's derivation in Bulgarian, with the rate it was billed at
**Did** — Each line of `GET /api/money/units/{unitId}/statement` now also carries `rateMinor` (the per-count rate, or a metered line's price per unit of measure; null for a share of a total) and `derivationBg`. `derivationBg` is the Bulgarian derivation, worded at read time from the issued line plus its run's stored `basis` (`DerivationBg.of`), so bills issued before this change read in Bulgarian too. The stored English `derivation` is unchanged (PM-FEE-015). Tariff lines are matched by stream + item and metered lines by consumption item. The business multiple is the adopted `businessMultiplier`, otherwise the frozen `BUSINESS_USE_MULTIPLIER_MIN`, never on REPAIR_FUND or metered lines. With no matching basis line both fields are null, never a guess. Amounts are formatted from integer minor units (`DerivationBg.euro`, "1 200,00 €"). The change is additive; OpenAPI is regenerated. Owner's D1 (a): a share of a total reads "дял от <total> по <key> · <count>".
**Rules covered** — PM-FEE-018, PM-FEE-016
