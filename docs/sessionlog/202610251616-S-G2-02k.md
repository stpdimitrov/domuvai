## S-G2-02k · 2026-10-25 · assembly — PROVISIONAL → FINAL

**Did** — A decision's status is derived when it is read, as a pure function `standing(decided day, protocol posted day, today)` (ADR-001). The stored row is never edited. A decision is PROVISIONAL until two windows have closed: the absentee window, day 7 after its sitting (PM-VOTE-013 / PM-GA-017), and the annulment window, 30 days after the protocol's posting (PM-GA-023). From the next day it is FINAL. Each window's last day comes from `statutoryDeadline` (PM-SYS-005). If the protocol's posting is not entered, there is no countdown and the decision stays PROVISIONAL. New act `POST …/assemblies/{a}/protocol-posting {postedOn}` (migration `V…__protocol_posting.sql`): once per assembly, by whoever runs it (RECORD_VOTE line, PM-SEC-001), on a day from the opened sitting's day to today. It is insert-only (UPDATE/DELETE do nothing, TRUNCATE is refused). The decision read now carries `status` (derived), `absenteeCloses`, `protocolPostedOn`, `annulmentCloses` and `finalOn`. In law: `GA_ABSENTEE_DAYS` = 7 (PM-GA-017) and `GA_ANNULMENT_DAYS` = 30 (PM-GA-023), both verified.
