## S-G2-02m · 2026-10-25 · assembly — the absentee merge

**Did** — New act `POST …/agenda/{item}/absentee-merge`, by whoever runs the assembly (RECORD_VOTE), on an item tagged for absentee ballots, once day 7 is over. It tallies the votes in effect and every absentee ballot against the majority the item was bound to. The absent owners' parts add to the represented share. It stores the result once in the new insert-only `assembly.merged_decision` (UPDATE and DELETE do nothing, TRUNCATE is refused), superseding the provisional decision, which stays as stored. The merged row has its own basis (naming the superseded decision and its hash, every vote, every ballot with its scan's hash, every holding present), basis_hash, law_version and engine_version. `GET …/decision` now shows the merged numbers and a `supersedes` id. `standing()` takes `eligible` and `mergedOn`: an eligible item is FINAL only once merged.
