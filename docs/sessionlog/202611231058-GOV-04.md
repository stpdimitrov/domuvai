**Did** — `RecordMandate.chair` marks one board member's (MB) mandate the chair of the management board, from that mandate's own protocol, when it is recorded and never after. `MandateAdministration.record` refuses the mark on any other office (400), and refuses a chair whose days in force would meet another chair's (409, `MandateChairTaken`, naming PM-FUND-004), before anything is stored. `Mandate.overlaps` reads days in force through `endsOnRecord`, as everything else does.
