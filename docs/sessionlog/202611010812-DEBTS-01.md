## DEBTS-01 · 2026-11-01 · money — a unit's open debts by debt date (the API half of #332, claim #421)

**Did** — `GET /api/money/units/{unitId}/open-debts?asOf=` lists what a payment valued on that date can settle, the oldest first: `debtDate`, `dueOn` and `outstandingMinor`, no more. It is `Ledger.openDebts`, the list the allocation works from, sorted, so a date it offers is never one a designation is refused for. `dueOn` comes from `ArrearsService.termOn`, the seam the arrears read uses, so there is no second lookup and no number. Read as the unit's arrears are (`MoneyAccess.unitCharges`, READ_UNIT_CHARGES): no new Action, no `Policy.MATRIX` change. The generated OpenAPI and web client follow; `tools/build_openapi.py` cites PM-DEBT-008, PM-DEBT-002 and PM-SEC-002.

**Rules covered** — PM-DEBT-008, PM-SEC-002. PM-DEBT-002 for `dueOn`, unchanged: the debt's date stands in for the announcement, as it does in the arrears read.

**Tests added** — `ArrearsServiceTest`, six: oldest first with a settled debt left out; the due day is the arrears read's, by the term on the debt's own date; the list is the allocation's and a debt raised after the date is not offered; a credit dated after the read date has closed its debt here while the arrears of that date still count it; postings with nothing open is an empty list; an open debt before any term stops the read naming the constant, a debt not offered is not looked up. `ArrearsWebTest`, two, and two extended: three fields and no more; a refused reader reaches no read; a malformed `asOf` is a 400; a missing term is a 500. `ArrearsIT`, two: every offered date is designated by a real payment, then the list is empty; a past date against the arrears of that date. `MoneyAccessIT`, one: owner, offices, cashier, a login tied to nobody, and a unit with nothing posted.
