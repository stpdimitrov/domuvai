## MINE-01 · 2026-11-22 · registry — the units of the person signed in (#485, claim #486)

**Did** — `GET /api/registry/me/units?on=` lists the asker's own titles in force on `on` (OWN and USR only), each with the entrance's label, the unit's designation and type, the asker's own `share`, `idealParts` (unit parts × share, exact) and `idealPartsSource`. The party is the one `WhoAsks` ties the sign-in to, never a parameter. `on` defaults to `LocalDate.parse(toSofiaDate(clock.instant()))`. The filter is a repository query by party (`TitleRepository.findByPartyId`), so no other person's title is read. Titles of one role over one unit are summed into one row. Nobody asking is 401. A login tied to no party gets an empty list and the tables are not opened. A bad `on` is 400. Rows are sorted by entrance label, then designation. No `Policy.MATRIX` change, no schema change. The endpoint is catalogued in `tools/build_openapi.py` (PM-ORG-011, PM-ORG-005, PM-SEC-002, PM-BOOK-011); `openapi.json` is regenerated.

**Rules covered** — PM-ORG-011, PM-ORG-005, PM-SEC-001, PM-SEC-002, PM-BOOK-011, PM-SYS-004.
