## S-G2-02a · 2026-10-12 · assembly + policy — the register: who is present at a sitting, and a remote participant identified before they count (PM-GA-016, PM-GA-012 in part)

**Shipped** — the first slice of quorum and sessions. A register of who is present at a sitting of an assembly: a line is a person present, in person or by video, entered by whoever runs the assembly, signed in. A person's presence brings themself, if they own in the entrance that day, and every principal whose proxy registered for this assembly names them — read from the book and the proxies each time, never copied. A participant by video is on the register and brings nobody until a verification of their identity is recorded — who verified, how, when — as its own insert-only record. Keeping the register is a new action of the policy's matrix (`KEEP_REGISTER`, version 9). Three operations: enter a person, record a verification, read the register. Nothing is weighed yet.

**The cut of S-G2-02** — a (this): the register · b: weighing quorum and opening the first sitting (PM-GA-012, its number in `law`) · c: the ladder — an hour later, the following day — and the count of sittings (#189) (PM-GA-013, PM-GA-014) · d: the higher quorum where one person owns more than half (PM-GA-015) · e: the yearly assembly (PM-GA-001).

**Verified** — `AttendanceServiceTest` (5) over a faked book and proxies; `AttendanceWebTest` (3) with tokens signed by the test's own key over the real `WhoAsks`, `Policy`, `Acting` and `AssemblyActs`; `AttendanceSignInOffWebTest` (1); `PolicyTest` pins the matrix at version 9. `AttendancePersistenceIT` (2) on this machine's Postgres 16 from a scratch copy, none skipped: an owner present in person brings herself and the owner who appointed her; an owner by video brings nobody until identified; a stranger is not entered; an owner, the controller and nobody at all do not keep the register; a line and a verification are neither rewritten nor removed. Nine mutants killed: an unidentified remote participant counted · counted but bringing nobody ignored · a non-owner bringing themself · a proxy nobody signed in for counted · anybody entered · the register open before the day · a person in person "verified" · the UTC day for the Sofia day · the register kept by anybody the policy allows, without running the assembly.

**Decisions** — mine, stated on #206: PM-GA-012 is not claimed here — the meter and the threshold are slice b's; the tests named for it prove "present in person or by proxy" · only an owner or a registered proxy's representative is entered · the register opens on the day the assembly meets · whom a line brings is worked out when it is read, so an owner who sells that day stops counting · a proxy from before proxies named their sign-in brings nobody.

**Open** — a participant cannot check themself in: the register is the keeper's · nobody leaves a sitting once entered · a holder of a right of use is not entered, as they are not weighed (the standing answer, pending counsel) · reading the register is open to anyone the api lets in · still waiting on the owner (S-G2-01h): the successor question, the recorder as proxy, the signer on the posting day.
