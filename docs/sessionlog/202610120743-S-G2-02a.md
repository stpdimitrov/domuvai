## S-G2-02a · 2026-10-12 · assembly + policy — the register: who is present at a sitting, and a remote participant identified before they count (PM-GA-016, PM-GA-012 in part)

**Shipped** — the first slice of quorum and sessions. A register of who is present at a sitting of an assembly: a line is a person present, in person or by video, entered by whoever runs the assembly, signed in. A person's presence brings themself and every principal whose proxy names them — each only if they own in the entrance **on the day of the sitting**, whenever the register is read; only proxies for the whole agenda, registered before the line was entered, by somebody signed in. Nobody is present twice: an owner who gave a proxy is not entered beside their representative, and a person on the register gives no proxy. Once somebody is on the register the assembly's date, place and agenda no longer change. A participant by video is on the register and brings nobody until a verification of their identity is recorded — who verified, how, when — as its own insert-only record. Keeping the register is a new action of the policy's matrix (`KEEP_REGISTER`, version 9). Three operations: enter a person, record a verification, read the register. Nothing is weighed yet.

**The cut of S-G2-02** — a (this): the register · b: weighing quorum and opening the first sitting (PM-GA-012, its number in `law`) · c: the ladder — an hour later, the following day — and the count of sittings (#189) (PM-GA-013, PM-GA-014) · d: the higher quorum where one person owns more than half (PM-GA-015) · e: the yearly assembly (PM-GA-001).

**Verified** — `AttendanceServiceTest` (9) over a faked book and proxies; `AttendanceWebTest` (3) with tokens signed by the test's own key over the real `WhoAsks`, `Policy`, `Acting` and `AssemblyActs`; `AttendanceSignInOffWebTest` (1); `PolicyTest` pins the matrix at version 9. `AttendancePersistenceIT` (2) on this machine's Postgres 16 from a scratch copy, none skipped: an owner present in person brings herself and the owner who appointed her; an owner by video brings nobody until identified; a stranger is not entered; an owner, the controller and nobody at all do not keep the register; a line and a verification are neither rewritten nor removed. Twenty mutants killed: whom a line brings read on the day of reading instead of the sitting's · an owner entered beside their representative · a holder of use brought by proxy · a proxy for listed items counted · a proxy handed over after the line counted · a participant verifying themself · a channel the assembly does not meet in · the register kept on a later day · the agenda changed after the register opened · a present person giving a proxy · and the first nine: an unidentified remote participant counted · counted but bringing nobody ignored · a non-owner bringing themself · a proxy nobody signed in for counted · anybody entered · the register open before the day · a person in person "verified" · the UTC day for the Sofia day · the register kept by anybody the policy allows, without running the assembly.

**Review** — fresh context, security-minded, seven findings — the register is what quorum will be summed from, so each would have become a wrong quorum. Taken: **the register of a past sitting changed after the sitting** — whom a line brought was read on the day of reading, so a sale the day after the meeting altered who had been present; it is the day of the sitting now, and the register is kept on that day only · **a principal could be present twice**, as themself and through their representative · a line brought holders of use and owners who had sold, through a proxy · **lines survived a reschedule** — a convenor could gather a register, move the meeting and open the new date on the old attendance; once somebody is on the register the date, place and agenda no longer change · a participant could verify their own identity, and attend by a channel the assembly does not meet in · the two tables refuse `TRUNCATE`, and a line and its verification are tied to the assembly's own entrance. One mutant survived the first run (the day of reading for the day of the sitting) and got the case that kills it.

**For counsel** (strictest reading built, `TODO(legal)` in `Attendance.kt`) — whether a proxy limited to listed agenda items makes its principal present for quorum (built: no) · whether a proxy handed over after its representative was entered counts at that sitting (built: no) · what "reliable identification" of a remote participant requires (built: another who runs the assembly records how; nobody verifies themself).

**Decisions** — mine, stated on #206: PM-GA-012 is not claimed here — the meter and the threshold are slice b's; the tests named for it prove "present in person or by proxy" · only an owner or a registered proxy's representative is entered · the register is kept on the day the assembly meets, and on no other · whom a line brings is worked out when it is read, but always as of the day of the sitting · a proxy from before proxies named their sign-in brings nobody.

**Open** — a participant cannot check themself in: the register is the keeper's · nobody leaves a sitting once entered · a holder of a right of use is not entered, as they are not weighed (the standing answer, pending counsel) · reading the register is open to anyone the api lets in · still waiting on the owner (S-G2-01h): the successor question, the recorder as proxy, the signer on the posting day.
