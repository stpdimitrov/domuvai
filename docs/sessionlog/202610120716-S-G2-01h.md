## S-G2-01h · 2026-10-12 · assembly — the office that convened runs the assembly; its convenor may record a written proxy (PM-GA-002, PM-GA-007, PM-GA-009)

Another office never touches it; an owners' assembly stays with its convening owner. **A proxy** is appointed by the principal signed in, for themselves, or recorded by whoever runs the assembly for the principal a written authorisation names — with the document's SHA-256 and who recorded it on the row.
