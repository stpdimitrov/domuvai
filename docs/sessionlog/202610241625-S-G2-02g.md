## S-G2-02g · 2026-10-24 · assembly — an item closed, its votes tallied into a stored decision

**Did** — `POST …/assemblies/{a}/agenda/{item}/decision` (`DecisionService`, `DecisionActs`, `DecisionController`). Whoever runs the assembly closes an item at the opened sitting, on its day. The votes in effect (the rows nothing reverses) are tallied on ideal parts against the majority the item was bound to, resolved through `law`'s `majorityRuleOn`. Each unit's cast per choice is capped at the unit's ideal parts. The decision is stored `PROVISIONAL`. The pure function `tally` compares exactly, FOR × 100 against threshold × base, where the base is the sitting's `represented` for REPRESENTED and all ideal parts for TOTAL. It writes the result line to 2 decimals and names its denominator. After the close, a vote or a correction on the item is a 409 (PM-VOTE-014), and so is a second close. The migration `V202610241620__decision_tallied.sql` makes the tallies exact numeric and adds: abstain, comparison, represented, outcome, basis/basis_hash, assembly, who closed it and their sign-in. It adds one decision per item (`decision_once_per_item`) and no-update and no-delete rules.

**Split** — the slice as briefed (close + tally + the reads) did not fit ~400 lines. The reads are **S-G2-02h**, which carries the owner's decision of 2026-10-24: "who may read how each owner voted — the manager, the board and the controller only; everyone else sees the result." So S-G2-02h adds a new matrix action for individual votes (BM, MB, CTL, with a version bump) and reads the decision under `READ_ASSEMBLY`. This slice adds no policy line. Closing uses `RECORD_VOTE`'s line through `AssemblyActs.runBy`.

**Rules covered** — PM-VOTE-002, PM-VOTE-011, PM-VOTE-012 (`DecisionTallyTest`), PM-VOTE-016, PM-VOTE-014 at close, PM-VOTE-011 for an owner not weighed, and PM-SEC-001 for closing (`VotePersistenceIT`).

**Verified** — `DecisionTallyTest` (3 tests) and `VotePersistenceIT` (8, of which 4 are new) pass locally against Postgres 16 under Testcontainers. Nine mutants were killed (`tools/mutate.py`): MORE_THAN taken as AT_LEAST; TOTAL weighed against represented; no unit cap; passing decided on the rounded share; reversed votes counted; a second close; a vote after close; a late arrival's vote tallied; FINAL at close. A first spelling of the rounded-share mutant did not compile and was rewritten. Gates: `tools/check.sh assembly`. Fresh-context review (Sonnet `zues-reviewer`) found one blocking issue: a decision could be deleted and its item closed again. Minor findings: the per-choice cap is now documented in `tally` (a split unit may count the rounding hair Holdings tolerates); the no-update rule means FINAL is a later row (Open); the result-line format is deliberate (the rule's text says "e.g."); the PM-VOTE-002 binding is asserted by the IT's `GENERAL@2009-01-01`; parsing `basis` is noted under Open.

**Decisions** — the owner's, 2026-10-24, quoted above (built in S-G2-02h). Mine:
- The status at close is `PROVISIONAL`. PM-VOTE-013 says "Provisional result is labelled `PROVISIONAL` until day 7 closes", and PM-GA-023 says "after 30 days status becomes `FINAL`". Neither makes a decision FINAL at close.
- Pass/fail is decided on the exact numbers, not the rounded 2-decimal report.
