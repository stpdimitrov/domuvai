## WEB-50 · 2026-10-29 · web — entrances and parties registered through the web; the empty-list words (#363)

**Did** — a firm screen `/registry` („Регистър на входове и лица“, in the firm sidebar) with two forms: „Регистриране на вход“ (`POST /api/registry/entrances`: label, how it is run, a new building's address or the building it joins) and „Регистриране на лице“ (`POST /api/registry/parties`: full name, optional id type and value). `lib/registry.ts`: `entranceOf`, `partyOf`, `entranceRefused`, `partyRefused`; `MANAGEMENT_FORM` and `ID_TYPE`, the closed lists the spec leaves as bare strings. Every form is offered to everyone and the API's 403 is shown in its words (the owner's Q1). #363: the 19 screens that said „Няма регистриран вход. Регистрирайте…“ now render one `NoEntrance` component.

**Rules covered** — PM-SEC-001 (mechanism: who may register is the API's), PM-ORG-001 (an entrance founds a building or joins one), PM-BOOK-011 (the identity number goes to the API and is neither kept in the form nor shown back), PM-BOOK-005 (the party must exist before a title; the form is the means, titles are WEB-51).

**Tests added** — `web/lib/registry.test.ts`, 4 tests named by rule ID: both closed lists read from the code and pinned (`management_form`'s CHECK in `V1__init.sql`, `IdType` in `Party.kt`); the forms as typed; the idValue dropped from the kept values and the success line; the 403 in its words, no role gate, two `unknownOutcome` branches, the sidebar label equal to no card heading, the buttons not substrings of one another; the old words gone from all 19 screens and `NoEntrance` used by all 19, telling nobody to register. E2E `registry_screen` (CI, last, so nothing above reads the new entrances): the board member (`ivan`) registers a party and is refused an entrance; `stefan` is refused both; `admin` registers an entrance and a second one joining its building, reads both on the entrance screen where `ivan` does not see them; a login `nobody` is told it holds no role.

**Leaves the seeded demo as it is** — a registered party holds no title and no login; a registered entrance holds no unit and nobody's mandate, and is named `ЯЯЯ e2e <hex>` so it sorts last. No seeded standing, list or figure moves, and `registry_screen` runs after every check that reads them.

**Review** — `zues-reviewer` (Sonnet), fresh context: one blocking, applied — the second entrance joined the first one's building under the same label, which `UNIQUE (condominium_id, label)` refuses; it has its own label now. Minors applied: a failed administrator step falls through to the no-role check instead of returning; a refused party (bad id type) is checked not to show the identity number back. Left: the PM-BOOK-011 unit test is a source scan (the e2e line is the behavioural check); the two-wording split waits on an administrator flag in `/me`.

**Decisions** — the owner's, 2026-10-29: "Confirm both" (every form shown to everyone; split: units are WEB-50b, titles WEB-51). Mine: **one wording, not two, for the empty list.** The contract wanted „nothing registered“ for the administrator and „no role“ for anyone else, from a session administrator flag; WEB-49 left none (`GET /api/identity/me` is "who, never what they may do", and the session holds the subject only). The empty list is both cases, so `NoEntrance` says both and tells nobody to register one — which is what #363 asks. If the owner wants the two split, `/me` would have to say who the administrator is (an API change, ADR-002).

**Open** — party ids are still typed (#300); no party list exists, so a registered party is read back by its number only. Units: WEB-50b. Titles: WEB-51.
